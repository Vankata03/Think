## Common questions

**Where is my journal stored?**
On your device only. Think has no server and no account. See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.html).

**I missed a day — is my streak gone?**
The streak resets, but nothing else is lost. Begin again; the practice matters more than the number.

**How do I change the daily notification time?**
Profile → Settings → Daily line notification.

**Does the Apple Watch app need the iPhone nearby?**
No. The watch timer runs on the watch itself, and completed sessions count toward the watch's own progress. Watch and iPhone progress are tracked separately for now.
